What's inside

Everything a small warehouse actually needs

No modules to configure and nothing to grow into later — six focused screens that cover the day-to-day of running stock.

Live dashboard

Revenue, low-stock counts, and top sellers at a glance, with a 7-day sales chart and stock value by category.

Product catalog

SKU, category, pricing, and unit tracking per product, with a reorder level and suggested reorder quantity built in.

Supplier directory

Contact details and addresses linked directly to the products they supply, so a reorder alert already knows who to call.

Movement audit trail

Every sale, purchase, adjustment, and write-off is logged as a movement record — a full history of who changed what.

Sales recording

Multi-item invoices with customer details, payment method, and discounts — stock decrements automatically on save.

Restock alerts

Fires automatically the moment stock drops to the reorder threshold, complete with a suggested reorder quantity.

How it fits together

Three steps, then it runs itself

Set the catalog up once — after that, alerts do the watching.

Add products & suppliers

Set a reorder level per product and link it to the supplier who restocks it.

Log sales & movements

Every sale, purchase, or manual adjustment updates stock and writes to the audit trail.

Get restock alerts

When stock crosses the threshold, an alert appears with the supplier and quantity already filled in.
